Our Services

Wallpaper

Wallpaper Services

Precise, damage-free work across every stage of the wallpaper process — installation, removal, and repair.

Wallpaper Installation

Whether it's a bold accent wall or a full-room statement, we install traditional and peel-and-stick wallpaper with sharp seams and precise pattern matching.

  • Precise measuring & pattern matching
  • Traditional & peel-and-stick papers
  • Accent walls to full-room installs
  • Clean, dust-minimized process

Wallpaper Removal

Old or damaged wallpaper removed safely and completely, with the wall left properly prepped for what comes next — paint or new paper.

  • Steam & solution-based removal
  • Wall damage assessment & prep
  • Safe for drywall & plaster
  • Debris-free cleanup

Wallpaper Repair

Seams lifting, bubbles forming, or a section water-damaged? We patch and repair wallpaper so it looks seamless — no need to redo the whole wall.

  • Seam & bubble repair
  • Water damage patching
  • Pattern-matched replacement panels
  • Touch-ups for high-traffic areas
