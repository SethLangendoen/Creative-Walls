Our Story

Meet Francis Domingo

Francis Domingo is the owner, designer, and installer behind Creative Walls 2.0. With 15+ years of hands-on experience and 500+ rooms transformed, he brings a designer's eye and an installer's precision to every project — from a single accent wall to an entire commercial hallway.

Creative Walls 2.0 specializes in wallpaper installation, removal, and repair, along with interior painting, for both residential and commercial spaces across Calgary and surrounding areas. Whether it's a bold mural, a textured feature wall, or a fresh coat of paint, the goal is the same: clean seams, sharp lines, and a finish that holds up.

Every project starts with a free estimate. We'll look at your space, talk through pattern and finish options, and give you a clear plan for timing and cost before any work begins — then leave the job site as tidy as we found it.
